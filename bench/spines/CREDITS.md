# Bench fixtures

The `real-*` images are crops of freely licensed photographs from Wikimedia
Commons. Each crop is one spine (or one book face) taken from the shelf band of the
original at about 1400 px tall — roughly what a phone at 1920×1080 sees up close.
Labels are the title as printed on the spine, which is what the app needs to resolve
a book — not the author or imprint.

CC BY-SA 4.0 requires attribution and share-alike: the `real-law-*` crops are
adaptations of that photograph and carry the same licence. The CC0 crops carry
no conditions. The `noise-*`, `sideways`, `angled` and title-named images are
synthetic, generated for this benchmark, and carry no third-party rights.

These are fixtures for measuring OCR, not shipping artwork.
